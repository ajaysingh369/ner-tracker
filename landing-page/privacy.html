Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2026

RunAstra ("we", "our", "us") is a mobile fitness application operated by Athleon under the website athleon.co.in. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect user information when you use the RunAstra application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect and process the following categories of information:

  • Basic profile information such as name, city, profile preferences, and chosen Astra mascot.
  • Fitness and activity data from Google Health Connect.
  • Optional fitness activity data from Strava.
  • Challenge participation and progression data.
  • Device information, app usage information, and advertising identifiers.

2. Google Health Connect Integration

RunAstra integrates with Google Health Connect to provide core fitness tracking functionality.

With your explicit permission, the application may request access to:

  • Step Count data
  • Distance data

RunAstra may read and write Step Count and Distance data through Google Health Connect in order to:

  • Track daily activity progress
  • Calculate personalized fitness goals
  • Power the Astra Zenith AI mascot and motivational features
  • Display wellness insights and challenge progress

Health Connect data is processed primarily on your device wherever possible.

We do NOT:

  • Sell Health Connect data to third parties
  • Use Health Connect data for advertising purposes
  • Share Health Connect data with ad networks
  • Use Health Connect data for data brokering

Health Connect data is used strictly to provide the app’s fitness tracking and wellness features.

3. Strava Integration

Users may optionally connect their Strava account using Strava OAuth authentication.

When connected, RunAstra may fetch read-only activity information from Strava, including:

  • Recent activity distance
  • Pace and timing information
  • Heart rate data (if available)

This information is used solely to provide the Runner Sync feature and related analytics inside the app.

Users may disconnect their Strava account at any time through app settings or directly through their Strava account permissions.

4. Advertising and Monetization

RunAstra uses third-party advertising services including Google AdMob and mediation partners such as Meta Audience Network to display advertisements.

These advertising providers may collect certain information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device identifiers
  • App interaction and usage data
  • General location information

This data may be used to provide personalized and relevant advertisements.

We do NOT share Google Health Connect data or sensitive fitness health data with advertising networks.

You can learn more about these providers here:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

5. Backend Data Storage

Some application data is securely stored on our backend infrastructure hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS).

This may include:

  • Name and profile details
  • City/location preferences
  • Selected Astra mascot
  • Challenge participation and progression data
  • Leaderboard and event participation information

We implement reasonable security practices to protect stored data against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

We may share limited information with:

  • Cloud hosting providers such as AWS
  • Advertising and analytics providers
  • Third-party integrations explicitly authorized by the user

Any such sharing is limited to the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

7. Data Retention

We retain user information only for as long as necessary to provide the services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data by contacting us.

8. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw permissions granted to integrations such as Health Connect or Strava

9. Children's Privacy

RunAstra is not intended for children under the age required by applicable local laws without parental consent.

10. Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect user information. However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be posted on athleon.co.in with a revised effective date.
