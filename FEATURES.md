# RunAstra Product Feature Map

This document tracks the core value propositions and unique features of RunAstra. It is intended for use in investor decks, stakeholder presentations, and product roadmap planning.

## 🚀 Unique AI & Engagement Features (The "Killer" Features)

### 1. Astra Zenith (Ambient AI Mascot)
- **Concept:** A dynamic mascot that "lives" in the step ring, responding to user performance with distinct personas.
- **Personas:** 
  - **Steady:** *The Zenith Monk* (Calm, disciplined guidance).
  - **Growth/Surge:** *The Warrior/Furious Boy* (Intense, high-energy challenges).
  - **Victory:** *The Astral Titan* (Grand applause for Zenith achievement).
- **Psychology:** Uses "Micro-interactions" and "Ambient Taunting" (reverse psychology) to nudge users. He "peeks" out to challenge users on first launch and when progress is lagging.
- **Value:** Transforms a static metric into a relationship. Increases daily retention through personality and emotional range.
- **Status:** Fully Implemented (Showcase Mode Active).

### 2. Astra Architect (Smart Missions)
- **Concept:** Long-term progression missions (7-day commitments) tailored to the user's fitness tier.
- **Value:** Moves beyond daily habits into "strategic commitments." Bridges the gap between casual walking and elite fitness (e.g., Foundation 5K, Elite 12K).
- **Status:** Fully Implemented.

### 3. Weather Adaptive Suggestions (India-Specific)
- **Concept:** Real-time tactical advice based on local city weather.
- **Value:** Deep empathy for the user's reality. Provides heatwave alerts, monsoon pivots (indoor walking suggestions), and humidity-based hydration reminders.
- **Status:** Fully Implemented (Mock service with major Indian cities; API-ready).

### 4. AI Event Narrator (The Viral Loop)
- **Concept:** Cinematic storytelling for major achievements.
- **Value:** Automatically generates a "Hero Narrative" upon challenge completion. Paired with a stylized **Digital BIB** glory card for high-impact social sharing (Instagram/WhatsApp).
- **Status:** Fully Implemented.

### 5. Fuel-Sync (AI Nutrition)
- **Concept:** Reactive nutrition advice triggered by activity intensity.
- **Value:** Detects high-intensity runs via Strava and suggests specific recovery protocols (protein/carb ratios) instantly.
- **Status:** Phase 1 Implemented.

### 6. Intelligent Home UX (Final Restructure)
- **Concept:** Task-prioritized interface hierarchy.
- **Value:** My Challenges (Missions) are prioritized at the top. Community "Upcoming Events" keep engagement high. "Exclusive Offers" (Sponsors) are non-intrusive at the bottom.
- **Empty State:** High-conversion "Explore Missions" card replaces generic empty lists.
- **Status:** Fully Implemented.

## 🛠 Core Infrastructure & Platform Features

### 7. Universal Health Sync (Fixed & Robust)
- **Detail:** Seamless background/foreground sync with Android Health Connect.
- **Morning Gap Fix:** Proactively syncs historical data (last 7 days) every 30 seconds, even before the user starts moving today.
- **SDK Robustness:** Multilayered parsing for different Android/Health Connect data structures (count, steps, units).
- **Status:** Fully Verified.

### 8. Strava Native Integration
- **Detail:** Full OAuth integration with Strava.
- **Value:** Displays recent athletic activities directly on the home screen, creating a unified fitness profile.

### 8. Digital Achievement BIBs
- **Detail:** Stylized, shareable posters for every activity and milestone.
- **Value:** High-quality PNG generation with QR codes for social proof and community growth.

### 9. Historical Granularity
- **Detail:** Deep-dive views into step and distance history (Weekly, Monthly, Yearly).
- **Value:** Allows users to visualize long-term progress and seasonal trends.

### 10. Native Ad Mediation (Google & Meta)
- **Detail:** Deep integration of Google AdMob using Native Advanced Ads.
- **Value:** Runs a silent auction between Google Ads and Meta Audience Network (via mediation) to display targeted ads. Instead of standard clunky banners, it extracts raw ad data (Title, Image, Subtitle) to render ads that look 100% identical to internal "Exclusive Offers," maximizing revenue without compromising UX.
- **Status:** Implemented (UI Ready, awaiting final AdMob IDs).

### 11. Sponsor & Community Engine
- **Detail:** Dynamic banner system and "Community Hero" spotlights.
- **Value:** Native monetization paths and social validation within the app, interleaved seamlessly with Google/Meta ads.
